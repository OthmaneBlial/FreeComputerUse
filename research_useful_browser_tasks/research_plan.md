# Useful browser tasks research

Research question: Which free public websites offer useful, realistic browser tasks
that can validate our DOM-first agent without affecting real people or accounts?

1. Consumer and public-data tasks: travel planning, product specifications,
   reference research and small document downloads. Verify primary website pages,
   visible interactions, free access, likely blockers and independent result checks.
2. Developer and business tasks: software evaluation, release/license research,
   model comparisons and public dataset discovery. Verify primary pages and design
   realistic tasks involving search, filters, tabs, navigation and extraction.
3. Product synthesis: prioritize useful tasks over toy controls, show verified
   sources in the styled public lab, and reproduce complex interactions in safe
   browser-only simulations. Actual agent validation is reported separately from
   source verification; no general success rate is inferred.

Each delegated topic uses at most five searches, records exact source links and
provides ready-to-paste goals, expected outputs, permission scope and an oracle.
No messages, purchases, real sign-ins/account creation, uploads, or real settings
changes. Avoid huge downloads and tasks requiring visual-only canvas control.
